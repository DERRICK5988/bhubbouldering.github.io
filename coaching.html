What coaching helps with

A better fit than one generic coaching card.

The coaching page should help users decide quickly, not guess. These use cases make that decision easier before they click book.

Use case

First-timer confidence

Best when you want a calmer intro, safer habits, better foot placement, and less confusion on the wall.

Use case

Technique cleanup

Useful for climbers who already climb regularly but want more efficient movement and cleaner body positioning.

Use case

Problem solving

Great when you want help reading sequences, managing attempts, and making better choices on actual projects.

Use case

Steady progression

Pick a coach style that fits your personality so feedback feels easier to apply from session to session.

Format 01

Starter session

  • Cleaner foot placement and route reading basics
  • Good if you want a supportive pace from the start

Format 02

Movement tune-up

Better for climbers who want sharper movement, less wasted energy, and clearer feedback on how they are moving between holds.

Technique focused Efficiency
  • Useful for smoother sequencing and body awareness
  • Helps turn “almost” sends into more repeatable movement

Format 03

Project support

A stronger fit when you want immediate on-wall feedback, better pacing between attempts, and stronger decision-making on harder climbs.

Live feedback Progression
  • Good for projecting, retries, and movement problem solving
  • Helps turn effort into clearer next steps instead of random attempts

FAQ

Questions users usually ask before booking.

A coaching page should answer the friction points early so booking feels easier.

Can complete beginners book coaching?

Yes. The coach selector is designed to make beginner-friendly options clearer, especially for people who want confidence, basics, and a calmer first experience.

What if I am not sure which coach suits me?

Coach 01 is live now for beginner-friendly sessions focused on confidence and movement basics. More coach profiles will be added soon.

Can coaching focus on a specific climbing problem?

Yes. A good coaching session can be built around real attempts, movement issues, or a specific style of climb you want help understanding better.

Do I need my own gear?

You can climb with your own gear, but Bhub also offers rental essentials on site, so users who are still starting out are not blocked by equipment.
