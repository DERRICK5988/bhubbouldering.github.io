First Timer?

What is bouldering? Bouldering is a friendly, social sport that gives you a full-body workout and sharpens your mind while you connect with others and have fun solving climbing challenges.

FAQs

Do I need to book?

No, just walk in. We’re open daily.

Is it kid‑friendly?

Suitable for kids 9+; ages 9 – 16 require guardian supervision.

Can I borrow shoes and chalk?

Yes, rental shoes and chalk are just RM11, making it easy to get started and enjoy your session

How long can I climb?

As long as you like, day passes are full‑day.

Can I bring pet?

No need! We’ve already got cats outside the gym, you can pet those.
