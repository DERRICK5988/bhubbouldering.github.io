First Timer?

What is bouldering? Rock climbing over soft foam mats up to ~4.5m. It’s accessible, thought‑provoking, social, and a great full‑body workout.

The Climbs

~80 problems rotated every ~5 weeks by our in‑house setters.

Problem Solving

Each climb has a “beta”. Need help? Ask our crew for tips.

BHub Grading

Follow the hold color and finish in control. Grades: B1 → Beast Tag. Wild Cards too!

FAQs

Do I need to book?

No—just walk in. We’re open daily.

Is it kid‑friendly?

Suitable for kids 9+; ages 9–16 require guardian supervision.

Can I borrow shoes?

Yes—rental shoes are RM11 per pair.

How long can I climb?

As long as you like—day passes are full‑day.
